Sophie Feltz

Final-year PhD student at the University of Birmingham.
Supervised by Prof. Gregor Leckebusch.

My research focuses on extreme winter windstorms, one of Europe’s most damaging natural hazards. Specifically, I am investigating how extreme windstorms cluster in both space and time on intra-seasonal timescales.

Beyond atmospheric science, I am passionate about science communication and widening participation in higher education. Throughout my PhD, I have delivered numerous outreach activities at primary schools in underrepresented communities.

I am also committed to open science; I openly share the code and data alongside my publications.
