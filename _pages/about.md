My research focuses on extreme winter windstorms, one of Europe's most damaging natural hazards. Specifically, I am investigating how extreme windstorms cluster in both space and time on intra-seasonal timescales.

Beyond atmospheric science, I am passionate about science communication and widening participation in higher education. Throughout my PhD, I have delivered numerous [outreach activities](/al-folio/outreach/) at primary schools in underrepresented communities.

I am also committed to open science; I openly share the [code](https://github.com/sophiefeltz) and [data](https://zenodo.org/search?q=metadata.creators.person_or_org.name%3A%22Feltz%2C%20Sophie%22&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=bestmatch) alongside my publications.
