Alongside my research, I have delivered outreach sessions to primary school children across Years 1-6 on the topic of weather.As a first-generation scholar, I work primarily with widening participation schools, where I hope to show young people from underrepresented backgrounds that STEM is for them. 

My outreach work has been supported primarily through the University of Birmingham's [BAM project](https://chembam.com/){:target="_blank"}, with additional support from the the [College of Life and Environmental Sciences](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/about/college-of-life-and-environmental-sciences/outreach){:target="_blank"}.

If you're based in the West Midlands and interested in arranging a session, please don't hesitate to get in touch.

## sessions

**Becoming a weather forecaster!** (Years 5-6)
An interactive session introducing pupils to meteorology. Through weather maps, pupils learn to identify and communicate key features of weather charts. Sessions have included hands-on experiments and a forecasting activity where pupils create and present their own forecast to the class.

**What is the weather like in the UK?** (Years 1-2)
A hands-on session designed to complement the KS1 curriculum on types of weather in the UK. Pupils become scientists for the day, exploring the diversity of UK weather through two experiments on ice melting and wind. They also have the chance to make their own fire-breathing dragon to explore wind speeds.
